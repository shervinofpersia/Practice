How to Use

Upload a Driving Video

3

Click the 🚀 Animate button below to generate, or click 🧹 Clear to erase the results

Tips for Best Results

  • Use clear, well-lit portraits for the source image
  • Ensure the driving video has good lighting and clear movements
  • For best results, use videos with similar head positions to your source image
  • Be patient during generation - high-quality animations take time to process
